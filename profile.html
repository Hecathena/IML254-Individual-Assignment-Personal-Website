Personal Life

I am the eldest of my siblings and I have three younger sisters. Me and my sisters are all capable of playing musical instruments, and we all have experiences in playing in orchestras. Two of my sisters can play the piano, one of them can play the guitar, and I can play the violin. Moreover, I was active in my chess club, orchestra, stem events, and I was a librarian. I often participate in competitions and events (willingly or unwillingly), but over the years I've learnt to enjoy them for the experience it brings.

Violin

I started learning the violin when I was 12 and had lessons up until I was 18. I ceased my lessons because I was entering university. Being in university made it hard to continue my lessons as all my materials and my violin were back in my hometown. I have a certification in ABRSM (Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music) and I have played in multiple events, including a wedding. I was also part of my school's orchestra and I have played in many events and gain lots of new experiences.

Japanese Language

I studied Science Stream Stem B in high school, and had the opportunity to learn Japanese. I have a UPBA (Ujian Pentaksiran Bahasa Asing) certificate for the subject and have been part of a student-exchange programme. I met the kindest people from that programme and we still keep in touch from time to time. Even though I am not actively learning Japanese at the moment, I still try to keep up with the language by watching anime and reading books in Japanese.
