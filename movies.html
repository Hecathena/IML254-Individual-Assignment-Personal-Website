My Favourites

Movies / TV Series / Anime / K-dramas / J-dramas

I watch almost anything

Movies

I mainly watch movies in English but occasionally I'll watch it in other languages if people recommend them to me or I find them interesting. I am willing to watch movies in a variety of genres, except horror. And I'm one of those people who put on English subtitles when watching English movies.

TV Series

I will refuse to watch TV series with more than 4 seasons. Anything more than that feels so overwhelming, especially if one season has up to 20 episodes. I might commit if I have watched the series right from season 1 and follow up as it gets renewed for a new season. And I will definitely watch it if it was adapted from a book.

Anime

I started watching anime around the same time I started learning Japanese. It started as a way to improve my vocabulary and pronounciation, but I started to love them when my friend began recommending me animes to watch. Among them are Bungo Stray Dogs, My Happy Marriage, and The Apothecary Diaries.

K-dramas

The long episode duration is often the thing that stands between me finishing a series or absolutely dropping it. This is often the case with K-dramas when one episode is an hour long so it takes me a while to get through them. When it comes to k-dramas, i mainly watch romances as they are enjoyable and they never fail to make me swoon. But as of right now, I'm trying to delve into C-dramas.

J-drama

I started watching J-dramas for the same reason I started watching anime: to enhance my Japanese language skills. Because my Japanese teacher advised me that if I truly wanted to improve my skills, I should watch J-dramas because the pronounciation and slang spoken between actors in a movie or series was much better than animes as the voice acting in animes are frequently exaggerated. Some great J-dramas and movies I've watched are Your Eyes Tell, And Yet, You are so Sweet, and A Calm Sea and Beautiful Days with You.
